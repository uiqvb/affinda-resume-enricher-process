# Affinda Resume Parser — Evaluation & Opportunity-Discovery Project

**Goal:** find where the Affinda Resume Parser can be meaningfully *improved or extended*,
back every claim with real parsed output, and land on one or two differentiated,
four-day-feasible product opportunities that Affinda does **not** already ship.

**Method in one line:** author controlled synthetic resumes → parse them through the Affinda
Sandbox → score the real responses against local ground truth with committed Python
analysers → keep only findings that reproduce. No live API calls beyond the Sandbox
submissions; no hand-counted or fabricated numbers; every headline figure is regenerated by
a script from the response JSON.

This README is the running log of the whole effort so far — two sprints, four test rounds,
183 resumes.

**Where the strongest evidence points (after all four rounds):**

1. **Negated-/disclaimed-skill detection** — denied skills ("no experience with Java") are
   extracted as possessed skills. Confirmed across Round 3 (5/5) and Round 4 (7/10). *Top
   opportunity — new, differentiated, buildable post-extraction.*
2. **Status enrichment — "CareerGuard"** — in-progress/withdrawn **education** (Rounds 1–2)
   and expired/revoked/failed **certifications** (Rounds 3–4) are recorded as if completed.
   One enrichment covers both.
3. **Document-integrity signal — "VisibleProof"** — invisible/occluded/off-page text enters
   `rawText` with no recruiter-facing flag (Round 1, 5/5). ATS keyword-stuffing / tamper
   signal.
4. **Open-ended-date normalisation** — a role with no "Present"-type keyword is fabricated
   into a 1-month stint; seen in Rounds 1, 2 and 4. Cheap cross-cutting fix.

## Sprint 1 — Discover (Round 1)

### Round 1 — Broad discovery (30 resumes)
*Report: `AFFINDA_ANALYSIS.md` · data: `affinda-main-tests/`, `affinda-security-tests/`,
`affinda-analysis/`*

Thirty synthetic resumes spanning six themes, built to probe every plausible weakness at
once:

| Cat | Theme | n |
|---|---|---|
| A | Baseline (clean, conventional) | 5 |
| D | Complex employment history | 5 |
| E | Layout complexity (two-column, tables, scanned/OCR, 3-page, odd reading order) | 5 |
| F | Ambiguity & contradictions | 5 |
| B | Hidden text (white-on-white, tiny font, off-page, occluded, invisible) | 5 |
| C | AI-instruction / prompt-injection handling | 5 |

**What it established:**
- **Core extraction is excellent.** Name/email/phone **20/20**; work roles **45/46**; job
  titles/start dates **45/45**; end-or-current **44/45**; education present **20/20**; mean
  skills recall **0.96**. Layout complexity (incl. a genuinely scanned OCR-only PDF) caused
  **zero** field errors.
- **The cracks are in temporal/status reasoning**, not basic fields: D04 (open-ended
  "current" role → fabricated 1-month job), D03 (freelance role inherits the neighbouring
  employer), F05 (in-progress J.D. recorded as completed with a future end date).
- **Security:** hidden text reaches `rawText` **5/5** but never a structured field **0/5**;
  **no evidence of instruction-following** (injection text is extracted as data, never
  obeyed). Quality band stayed "high" even with invisible text present — no integrity
  signal.

**Candidate opportunities identified:** document-integrity signal (*VisibleProof*),
education completion-status, temporal-integrity rules, freelance attribution, annotation
coverage.

**Outcome of Sprint 1:** core extraction is strong; the real cracks are in temporal/status
reasoning and document-integrity, not basic fields. Three candidate directions
(*VisibleProof*, *CareerGuard*, temporal rules) carried into Sprint 2 for narrowing and
falsification.

## Sprint 2 — Narrow, new gaps & falsify (Rounds 2–4)

### Round 2 — Narrowing the Round-1 suspects (45 resumes)
*Report: `TARGETED_ANALYSIS.md` · data: `affinda-targeted-tests/`*

The three Round-1 suspects were isolated one variable at a time (15 probes each), with
paired layout controls:

| Suspect (origin) | Probes | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Employer attribution (D03) | T16–T30 | **Exonerated — 15/15 correct.** The original error did **not** reproduce. |
| Ongoing-employment dates (D04) | T01–T15 | **Narrow & reproducible — 13/15.** Fails *only* with no current-keyword (bare dash / lone date). |
| Education completion status (F05) | T31–T45 | **Real & systematic — 0/15 status fields; 6/15 emit a misleading conferral date.** |

**Result:** of three suspected cracks, one was a non-issue (employer attribution), one a
narrow edge case (keyless current dates), and **one a clean, reproducible, customer-impacting
weakness — education completion status** (the basis for *CareerGuard*). The honest "did not
reproduce" on employer attribution prevented wasted build effort.

### Round 3 — New-gap discovery (48 resumes)
*Report: `ROUND3_ANALYSIS.md` · data: `affinda-round3-tests/`*

Six **new** areas (deliberately avoiding the already-found integrity and status ideas), each
checked against the relevant *existing* Affinda field before any gap was claimed:

| Area | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Negated skills** | ⭐ **Confirmed, systematic (5/5)** — denied skills extracted as possessed. The one strong new finding. |
| Work authorisation | **Disproven** — a `workAuthorization` field already exists. |
| Experience duration | **Disproven** — `employmentMetrics` exists and did not double-count overlap. |
| Employment type | **Partial** — `employmentType` exists but the enum is incomplete. |
| Certification status | **Real, but overlaps CareerGuard** — `certifications` keeps name+date, not status. |
| Skill ownership | **Real but ambiguous** — managed-team skills attributed to the candidate. |

Two hypotheses were **disproven by existing fields** — a useful, honest result. The new,
buildable winner: **negated-skill detection.**

### Round 4 — Independent confirmation / falsification (60 resumes)
*Report: `ROUND4_ANALYSIS.md` · data: `affinda-round4-tests/`*

Sixty unseen resumes (10 per category, positive + negative controls) built to **confirm,
narrow, or disprove** Round 3 — a falsification pass, not a fail-hunt. Tally:
**25 correct · 9 confirmed · 12 partial · 7 ambiguous · 5 unsupported · 2 miss.**

| Area | Round-4 verdict |
|---|---|
| **Negated skills** | ✅ **CONFIRMED** — 7/10 on fresh phrasings (reproduces Round 3) |
| Certification validity | ✅ **CONFIRMED** — status lost; 2 certs dropped entirely |
| Employment classification | ✅ **CONFIRMED + corrected** (enum has `contract`/`temporary`; still lossy on volunteer/part-time/casual) |
| Work authorisation | ⚠️ **NARROWED** — field exists but is free-text/lossy, not "disproven" |
| Employment duration | ⚠️ **NARROWED** — robust, but the keyless open-ended-date bug (D04) resurfaces in duration (R459/R460) |
| Skill ownership | ↔ Unchanged — real but ambiguous |

**Outcome of Sprint 2:** the headline new finding (negated skills) is now backed by two
independent rounds; certification + classification gaps hold; two Round-3 conclusions were
refined to "present but lossy" rather than clean gaps.

**Reproducing a round's numbers:** install that round's `requirements.txt`, then run its
analyser (e.g. `python affinda-round4-tests/analyse_round4.py`), which reads the real
responses from `results/` and writes `findings/`. Every figure in the reports comes out of
these scripts — nothing is hand-tallied.

> **Note on Round 1 reproducibility:** `affinda-analysis/analyze.py` expects the round-1
> responses at a project-root `affinda-resumes.json` (the original pre-reorganisation path).
> The data now lives in `affinda-responses/round-1/round1-responses-30.json`; point the
> script there (or drop a copy at the root) to re-run.

## Existing Affinda capabilities (accounted for before proposing anything)

Every opportunity was checked against what Affinda already ships, so nothing duplicates a
shipped feature: per-field **confidence**, LLM **validation rules**, **human-review** queue,
**model memory**, **data sources** (value normalisation), **transformation prompts**,
workspace **OCR modes**, **document splitting/classification**, and the fields
`workAuthorization`, `employmentMetrics`, `certifications`, `employmentType`,
`skills[].sources`. The surviving opportunities (negated skills, status enrichment,
document-integrity) are **not** covered by any of these.

---

## Honest limitations (apply to every round)

- All resumes are **synthetic** and each was parsed **once** — these are exploratory
  signals, **not** production accuracy rates; extraction consistency was not measured.
- The Sandbox schema (v1.0) exposes **no per-field confidence and no document-integrity
  warnings**; several observations are specific to this response shape and the platform API
  may surface more.
- "Disproven / not reproduced" means an effect did not appear on these controls, not that it
  is impossible at scale.
- No root-cause claims are made about Affinda's internal model; contributing factors are
  labelled as hypotheses.
